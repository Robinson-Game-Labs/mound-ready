How scoring works
  • 10 XP per mobility exercise, 15 per bonus drill.
  • +50 XP for finishing every mobility exercise; +30 for the full bonus block.
  • Every 14 full mobility days moves you up a phase (Foundation, Build, Load, Game Ready): more reps, longer holds, harder versions, and new exercises at Phases 2 and 3. Missed days don't count toward it.
  • The mirror drills change by day of the week to cover each part of the delivery from the guides.
  • Your streak adds 5% XP per day, up to +50%.
  • Finish 7 days in a row to earn a rain delay. A rain delay covers one missed day so the streak survives. You can hold 2.
  • Mobility velo is a game score that climbs with XP. It is not a radar reading of your fastball.
  • Every 30 days you get a report card to text your parent.
