# Mound Ready

A daily mobility app for a young pitcher. The routine is built around the components in the 3X Pitching Mechanics Guide (Vol. 1 & 2).

- **Daily mobility:** 9 exercises to start, about 12 minutes. Each has an illustrated how-to, a timer and a "why it matters" note.
- **Phases:** every 14 completed days moves him up (Foundation → Build → Load → Game Ready), with more reps, longer holds, harder versions, and new hamstring and lat work.
- **Bonus block:** mirror drills that rotate by day of the week across 12 parts of the delivery from the guides, or a recovery block on days he pitched.
- **Scoring:** XP, streaks, rain delays, a "mobility velo" level and 15 badges.
- **Sharing:** Instagram-story score cards and progress clips stamped with his stats.
- **30-day report:** a report card he texts to a parent at the end of each block.

Progress is saved on the phone it's used on (browser storage). No account and no server.

## Install on iPhone
Open the site in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Safety
Mobility should feel like a stretch, never pain. Get medical clearance before starting a program.
